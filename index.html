Featured · ACM Multimedia 2026 Poster

Beyond Visual Evidence: Revealing and Mitigating Relational Privacy Leakage in Document MLLMs

Accepted 10 July 2026 · ACM MM 2026 · Poster

Document multimodal language models can reveal correlated private fields even when visual evidence is weak. This work studies relation-level leakage and introduces DRUF, a dynamic unlearning method that suppresses exposed field pairs while preserving key information extraction.

Beining Xu*, Hairui Wang*, Jiaxin Wang, Changsheng Chen, Anirban Chakraborty.
