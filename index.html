Research papers and poster

ACM MM 2026 Poster · Accepted July 10, 2026

Beyond Visual Evidence: Revealing and Mitigating Relational Privacy Leakage in Document MLLMs

Beining Xu*, Hairui Wang*, Jiaxin Wang, Changsheng Chen, Anirban Chakraborty

Document MLLMs can reveal correlated private fields even when visual evidence is weak. We study relation-level privacy leakage and introduce DRUF, a dynamic unlearning method that suppresses exposed field pairs while preserving key information extraction.

Auditing Implicit Sycophancy in Mental-Health Dialogue: Structured Clinical-State Diagnostics and Clean Matched Benchmarks

Tianze Han*, Beining Xu*, Hanbo Zhang, Yongming Lu

This paper audits a hidden dialogue risk: a response may sound empathetic while reinforcing harmful cognition. It introduces a clean matched benchmark and Dynamic Emotional Signature Graphs (DESG) to evaluate clinical-state changes rather than surface wording alone.

TECP: Token-Entropy Conformal Prediction for LLMs

Beining Xu, et al.

A framework that uses token-level entropy as a logit-free and reference-free uncertainty measure and integrates it into a split conformal prediction pipeline.
